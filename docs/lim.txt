       【摘要】针对传统数字人制作流程成本高、周期长的产业瓶颈，本文以提升生产效率为核心
    目标，设计并实现了一套单目视频驱动的数字人快速生成和编辑系统。该方案创新整合了三维
    高斯泼溅高效渲染与易于编辑的优势，允许用户在短时间内生成高保真数字人后，对素材进行
    智能分割和存储，并立即进行直观、灵活的二次创作。测试结果显示，本系统能够显著缩短数字
    人的制作时间，并降低对专业采集设备与技能的依赖。研究表明，一体化生成和编辑系统是实
    现电影工业数字人生产降本增效的可行路径，可为海量三维素材库的构建和中小成本影片的视
    觉效果制作提供有力支持。

3D digital human generation and editing system for film production: Design
and application

◎ LI Mengtian (Shanghai Film Academy, Shanghai University; Shanghai Engineering Research Center of Motion Picture Special Ef‐
fects)
◎ YAO Shengxiang, YANG Yang (Shanghai Film Academy, Shanghai University)

Abstract: We introduce a novel system for the rapid generation and editing of digital humans from monocular video, de⁃
signed to overcome the significant bottlenecks of high cost and long production cycles found in traditional workflows . Our
approach is built upon 3D Gaussian splatting, capitalizing on its high⁃efficiency rendering and inherent editability. The re⁃
sulting pipeline enables users to quickly produce high⁃fidelity avatars, intelligently segment and archive the underlying as⁃
sets, and immediately proceed to intuitive, flexible editing. Our experiments confirm that the system accelerates the produc⁃
tion of digital humans and minimizes reliance on specialized hardware and expertise. The findings indicate that an inte⁃
grated generation and editing pipeline is a viable strategy for making digital human creation more efficient and cost⁃effec ⁃
tive for the film industry. Furthermore, our approach facilitates the development of extensive 3D asset libraries and empow⁃
ers high⁃quality visual effects production, even for films with limited budgets.
Keywords: Digital Human; Artificial Intelligence; Film Production; Neural Radiance Field; 3D Gaussian Splatting; DeepSeek
